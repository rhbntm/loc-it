# LOC IT — LOCTITE Digital Consumer Experience

> **Making LOCTITE easier to discover, easier to choose, and easier to buy.**

LOC IT is a digital experience concept developed during the **Henkel Philippines Hackathon 2026** for the business challenge:

> **How can LOCTITE become the most trusted adhesive brand among Filipino consumers?**

The concept focuses on one key gap in the consumer journey: even when a brand has strong product credibility, consumers still need to be able to **find it, understand which product fits their problem, and act on that decision easily.**

LOC IT was designed as the digital layer that helps bridge that gap.

---

## The Business Problem

LOCTITE has strong credibility as an adhesive brand, but consumer purchasing decisions are not based on product performance alone.

For everyday consumers, the decision can come down to:

* Which adhesive do I actually need?
* Will it work for my specific problem?
* How do I use it correctly?
* Where can I buy it?
* Why should I choose this instead of a cheaper, familiar alternative?

This creates friction between:

**“I need to fix this.”**

and

**“I bought LOCTITE.”**

LOC IT was designed to reduce that friction.

---

# The LOC IT Concept

LOC IT is a guided digital experience that helps consumers move from a **specific problem to a relevant LOCTITE product**.

Instead of requiring consumers to understand the adhesive category first, the experience starts with the consumer's situation.

### Consumer Journey

**Problem**

↓

**LOC IT**

↓

**Guided questions**

↓

**Recommended product**

↓

**Why this product?**

↓

**How to use it**

↓

**Where to buy**

↓

**Purchase**

This turns product information into a more actionable consumer experience.

---

# How It Supports the Campaign

LOC IT was developed as part of the proposed **#LOCINWITHLOCTITE** campaign.

The broader campaign aims to build trust through repeated proof across the consumer journey:

**Discover → Research → Decide → Purchase → Share → Discover again**

LOC IT primarily strengthens the **Research → Decide → Purchase** stages.

Marketing activities create awareness and interest.

LOC IT helps convert that interest into a confident product decision.

E-commerce then provides the transaction layer.

---

## The Role of LOC IT

LOC IT is **not intended to replace the existing LOCTITE website or become another marketplace.**

Its purpose is to provide a faster, more guided path for consumers who already have a problem they want to solve.

### Existing brand ecosystem

**Brand / Social / Search**

→ awareness

**LOCTITE digital content**

→ information

**LOC IT**

→ decision

**E-commerce / Retail**

→ purchase

**Reviews / UGC**

→ proof and advocacy

This makes LOC IT a **digital connective layer** within the larger campaign rather than an isolated website.

---

# Business Value

### 1. Reduce Decision Friction

Consumers do not necessarily want to study every adhesive product.

They want an answer to:

> **“Which one should I use?”**

LOC IT simplifies that decision.

### 2. Improve Product Discoverability

Instead of relying entirely on consumers already knowing the LOCTITE product range, the experience starts with the consumer's need.

### 3. Increase Purchase Intent

The recommendation experience ends with a clear path toward purchasing the selected product.

The goal is to reduce the distance between **product consideration and transaction.**

### 4. Make Product Knowledge More Accessible

Technical product differences are translated into consumer-oriented guidance:

**What are you fixing?**

**What material is involved?**

**What kind of repair is needed?**

→ **Which product is appropriate?**

### 5. Create a Measurable Digital Funnel

The experience can be measured across the consumer journey:

**Discovery → Guide Start → Recommendation → Purchase Click**

This provides a potential foundation for measuring campaign effectiveness rather than relying only on awareness metrics.

# Experience Highlights

## Guided Product Discovery

Consumers answer a small number of questions about their situation instead of manually searching through a large product catalog.

## Explainable Recommendations

The experience provides a recommended product together with the reasoning behind the recommendation.

The objective is not simply:

> **“Buy this.”**

It is:

> **“This is the product that fits your situation, and here's why.”**

## Usage Guidance

The recommendation experience connects product selection with practical usage information, helping consumers feel more confident about their purchase.

## Where to Buy

Once the consumer has made a decision, the experience provides a path toward available purchase channels.

The intended strategy is to **meet consumers where they already shop** rather than forcing them into an unfamiliar purchasing environment.

# Example Use Case

A consumer needs to repair a small plastic component.

Instead of searching through multiple adhesive products, they enter LOC IT and answer a few questions about the material and type of repair.

LOC IT then provides:

**Product Match**

→ **Why it fits**

→ **How to use it**

→ **Where to buy it**

The consumer moves from uncertainty to action with fewer decisions in between.
